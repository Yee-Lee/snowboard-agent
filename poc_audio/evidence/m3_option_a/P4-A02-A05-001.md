## A02 mapping basis

Across 18,240,000 samples per channel:

- channel 0 had 18,239,314 nonzero samples; channel 1 had zero;
- channel 0 container low-byte OR was zero;
- after arithmetic shift by 8, decoded low-byte OR was `0xff`;
- many values were inconsistent with right-aligned 24-bit sign extension;
- the reviewed formula is `float32(s32 >> 8) / 8388608`, followed by
  saturating S16 conversion.

This agrees with the existing wiring attestation that INMP441 L/R is tied to
GND and selects the left I2S channel. The known-signal basis is the 20 authorized
speech/pause Pilot clips whose representative labels were previously reviewed.
The analyzer emitted statistics only, not raw samples or transcripts.

Eighteen near-full-scale source samples were observed. They do not change the
bit mapping, but remain an input-transient risk for P4-A08 and Formal fixture
review.

## Retained failed A05 run and flush policy

The first exact-SHA streaming run is retained as `FAIL`: it produced 15,857 of
16,000 expected samples and a 177-sample partial because an empty final call did
not drain libsamplerate's filter state. No gate was changed and no output was
padded or truncated.

The corrected implementation computes the exact rational target from real
source samples. It supplied 429 zero-valued filter-drain inputs, tracked
separately from source audio, and recovered the 143-sample deficit exactly. It
fails if the drain returns anything other than the unique `floor(input / 3)`
output length. Every passing deterministic case reported drain 429, output
16,000, 50 full frames, and partial zero.

All runs ended with zero candidate processes, threads, file descriptors, and
ALSA owners. Raw WAVs, generated signals, logs, and target paths remain in the
controlled Git-ignored evidence directories.
